# Reel covers

Three Instagram reels sit on the Reviews page. Each shows a still until the visitor taps it, then Instagram's own player opens inside the frame. The still is therefore a poster, not a frame of the video, and it can be shot to match the rest of the site. Vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920, under 150 KB, no overlaid text, warm daylight or the treatment light, nothing blue in frame. Save in `public/placeholders/`.

What the reels contain, from the embeds: reel 1 (Cmq82Syh1UH) is a handpiece finishing the edge of an upper front tooth under the light; reel 2 (Ckal4a5AdlL, captioned "The try in") is a gloved hand lifting the lip on a full arch of veneers at try-in; reel 3 (Csi3X2ugr05) is a whitening session with the gum barrier on and gel being brushed onto the upper teeth.
