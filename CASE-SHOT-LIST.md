# Case photographs: eight pairs

Rules that apply to every pair

- Real patients of the clinic only, with written consent for publication on file. A "before" cannot be staged later; it comes from the clinic's photo records. The "after" can be re-shot at a review visit in the same framing.
- The personas below are casting notes to find the matching case in the archive. When you pick the real case, send me its true facts (age, teeth, visits, months since treatment, what was done) and I rewrite the story and the figures on the site to match.
- Standard clinical frontal view unless the case says otherwise: patient upright, head straight, occlusal plane horizontal, cheek retractors in, teeth slightly apart (or in occlusion where stated), camera at the level of the mouth, same distance and magnification for before and after, ring or twin flash (or the treatment light with no shadows), fixed white balance, no filters. Crop both images identically to the mouth or lower face only, no eyes, so nobody is identifiable.
- Files: `public/placeholders/case-NNN-before.jpg` and `case-NNN-after.jpg`, 4:3, about 1400 x 1050, under 120 KB each.

## Case 014, the hero. Eight upper veneers

Persona: Mariam, 34, an architect who presents to clients and had stopped smiling with her teeth showing in photographs. Small, slightly uneven upper front teeth with old composite on the two central incisors that had yellowed at the edges. She did not want a "Hollywood" result, only teeth that looked like hers at their best, one shade brighter. Eight veneers canine to first premolar on the upper arch, three visits in six days, photographed at the six-month review.

This pair is also the home page hero, shown full width, so shoot it as a natural smile rather than a retracted view.

- Before: `case-014-before.jpg`. Natural full smile, lower third of the face from just below the nose to the chin, lips framing the teeth, head straight, soft even light. Visible: short and uneven incisal edges, a slight rotation of a lateral incisor, yellowed composite edges on the centrals, upper teeth sitting back from the lip line. Neutral expression, no make-up change between the two shots.
- After: `case-014-after.jpg`. Same crop, same distance, same light and lip position. Visible: eight veneers with an even incisal edge that follows the lower lip, warm natural shade with translucency at the edges, no bulk at the gum line, healthy pink gums, lower teeth whitened to match.

## Case 009. Two lower implants

Persona: Hossam, 56, retired engineer who had chewed on one side for years after losing the lower left first and second molars. The neighbouring teeth had started to tilt into the gap. He was nervous about surgery and wanted to be told exactly what would happen. Two implants placed in one visit under local anaesthetic with a small graft on one side, crowns fitted four months later, photographed at the one-year review.

- Before: `case-009-before.jpg`. Lateral retracted view of the lower left side, mouth slightly open, taken with a cheek retractor and, if available, a side mirror. Visible: the empty space where the two molars were, the premolar in front tilting backwards, the gum ridge sunken, the upper molar above the gap slightly over-erupted. Keep the lower occlusal plane horizontal.
- After: `case-009-after.jpg`. Same side, same angle and magnification. Visible: two ceramic crowns filling the space with natural contours, gum fitting snugly around them, the tilted premolar now in contact with the first crown, clean margins, no visible metal.

## Case 011. Full mouth restoration

Persona: Samir, 62, a businessman with crowns from three different decades, worn front teeth and a bite that had dropped, waking most mornings with an aching jaw. He wanted to stop patching tooth by tooth. A five-week trial bite in temporary material, root canals where needed, two implants, then ceramic crowns and onlays fitted quadrant by quadrant over six months, photographed at eight months.

- Before: `case-011-before.jpg`. Frontal retracted view with the teeth together in the bite. Visible: short, worn front teeth with flat edges, several mismatched old crowns in different shades, one or two with a dark line at the gum, a lower face that looks over-closed, uneven gum heights.
- After: `case-011-after.jpg`. Same view, teeth together. Visible: restored front tooth length and a higher, even bite, uniform ceramics in one natural shade, clean gum margins, canines and premolars matching the front teeth. The whole arch should read as calm and consistent, not white.

## Case 017. Whitening and bonding on two front teeth

Persona: Nour, 27, works in marketing, chipped the corner of an upper central incisor years ago and had a small gap beside it, on teeth stained by coffee. She wanted a quick, reversible fix with no drilling. In-clinic whitening first, then composite layered on the two front teeth in the same visit, photographed at three months.

- Before: `case-017-before.jpg`. Close frontal retracted view of the six upper front teeth only, filling the frame, teeth slightly apart against a dark background (a black contraster behind the teeth if you have one). Visible: the chipped corner on the central incisor, the small gap beside it, a yellowish overall shade.
- After: `case-017-after.jpg`. Same close view and background. Visible: the corner rebuilt so that it is hard to find, the gap closed, a brighter but natural shade across all six teeth, polished composite surface with the same texture as the enamel.

## Case 021. Ten old veneers replaced

Persona: Dina, 43, a pharmacist whose veneers were done twelve years ago at another clinic and had become bulky, too long and dark at the gum line, with gums that bled when brushing. She wanted thinner, shorter teeth and healthy gums. The old veneers were removed, the gums left to heal for three weeks, then ten new veneers over three visits, photographed at fourteen months.

- Before: `case-021-before.jpg`. Frontal retracted view, teeth slightly apart. Visible: ten veneers that look thick and opaque, grey or dark lines at the gum margins, red and slightly swollen gum edges, front teeth longer than the smile line needs.
- After: `case-021-after.jpg`. Same framing. Visible: thinner veneers with natural translucency, clean margins that disappear under firm pink gums, shorter and better-proportioned front teeth, no dark lines.

## Case 023. Clear aligners

Persona: Farida, 24, a junior lawyer who did not want fixed braces in court. Crowded lower front teeth and one upper lateral incisor turned out of line. Twenty-two trays over eleven months, check-ups every eight weeks with photographs in between, a fixed wire behind the lower front teeth and a night retainer on top, photographed two months after the last tray.

- Before: `case-023-before.jpg`. Frontal retracted view, teeth slightly apart, both arches visible. Visible: overlapping lower incisors, the rotated upper lateral, a midline that is slightly off; the rest of the teeth healthy and unstained so the change reads as alignment only.
- After: `case-023-after.jpg`. Same framing. Visible: both arches aligned, the lateral in line with its neighbours, the midline centred, gums unchanged. If the lower fixed retainer is visible from the front it does not matter, but a frontal view normally hides it.

## Case 026. Fixed upper bridge on six implants

Persona: Fathy, 68, a retired teacher whose upper denture had been loose for years. He used adhesive every morning and had stopped eating out. He wanted something that did not move. Six implants in the upper jaw with a temporary fixed bridge on the same day, then a ceramic bridge of twelve teeth once the bone had healed, photographed at ten months.

- Before: `case-026-before.jpg`. Frontal view with the old denture in place, natural smile or lightly retracted. Visible: the flat, uniform pink acrylic of the denture, teeth that look set in a row and a little too even, the upper lip unsupported. (If the clinic's record only has the ridge without the denture, that is acceptable; tell me and I adjust the caption.)
- After: `case-026-after.jpg`. Same framing and distance. Visible: the fixed ceramic bridge with individual tooth shapes and a natural gum line, the lip supported, the smile line following the lower lip.

## Case 031. Six veneers closing gaps

Persona: Yasmin, 31, a teacher with two small upper lateral incisors and gaps on either side of them. Bonding done in the past had stained and never closed the gaps well. She wanted the gaps closed without ending up with big front teeth. Six veneers across the upper front, the laterals widened in proportion, three visits in one week, photographed at four months.

- Before: `case-031-before.jpg`. Close frontal retracted view of the upper six front teeth, teeth slightly apart. Visible: narrow, peg-shaped lateral incisors, dark gaps either side of each lateral, discoloured old composite on their edges, central incisors of normal size.
- After: `case-031-after.jpg`. Same close view. Visible: the laterals widened to a natural proportion, gaps closed, the centrals the same size as before, one even natural shade, clean margins.
